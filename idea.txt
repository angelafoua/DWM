Title

Why I like this design
Because it is low risk.
Even if parameter prediction fails, you still publish:
"These dataset properties influence DWM behavior."
That is a legitimate scientific result.
You don't need to prove you can automatically tune DWM yet. You only need to prove that measurable dataset characteristics are associated with parameter behavior. That is a much easier first paper and a solid foundation for the predictive work in later papers.
